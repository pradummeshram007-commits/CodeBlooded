## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, high-precision visual environment engineered for enterprise legal technology and multi-agent AI orchestration. The interface balances cinematic depth with institutional rigor, evoking the control room of an advanced intelligence network rather than a standard corporate SaaS tool. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- Deep, low-reflectance spatial planes that minimize cognitive fatigue while maximizing high-contrast legibility.
- Optical hierarchies defined by subtle luminosity, selective atmospheric blooms, and micro-grid textures rather than aggressive visual weight.
- Uncompromising technical precision: calibrated mono typography for ledger data, parsing hashes, and autonomous agent statuses, paired with an executive-grade geometric sans-serif for legal copy and structural navigation.
- High-stakes authority: interactions feel instantaneous, tactile, and mathematically composed, avoiding consumer-oriented whimsy, playful radiuses, or unnecessary motion.

## Colors

The palette is engineered natively for a dark-first environment, layering midnight-navy tones to build tactile optical depth while deploying spectral accents for immediate state comprehension.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#080B12`):** The foundational substrate. Deep midnight navy absorbing glare, framed against subtle structural viewport grids.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#101624`):** Secondary regions, sidebars, dock consoles, and inactive workspace viewports.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#151D2D`):** Elevated analytical cards, modal inspectors, and dynamic agent thread containers.
- **Primary Accent (`#8B5CF6`):** Electric Violet. Represents autonomous agent intelligence, orchestration threads, generative clause drafting, and machine-learning transformations.
- **Secondary Accent (`#43D9FF`):** Luminous Cyan. Used for analytical telemetry, edge networks, interactive highlights, and active telemetry focus.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - **Mint (`#5DE2B1`):** Cryptographic verification, audit passing, signed clauses, and low-risk contract parameters.
  - **Amber (`#FFBE69`):** Clause discrepancies, indemnification risks, and manual intervention flags.
- **Text & Contrast Matrix:**
  - High-tier text (`#F4F7FF`): Used for contract content, headline stats, and executive decisions.
  - Subdued text (`#9BA8BD`): Metadata, clause line numbering, timestamp arrays, and inactive labels.

## Typography

Typography establishes an analytical dialogue between high-level executive synthesis and raw contractual data.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Selected for display, headers, and dense contract reading. It maintains geometric precision without being sterile, providing open counters that stay legible under low-luminosity conditions.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Strictly reserved for agent status arrays, confidence scores, legal entity IDs, clause checksums, and token counters. All numeric legal thresholds and financial tables must render using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Display sizes leverage tighter letter-spacing for a tailored, commanding presence. Metadata labels use micro-caps or standard mono case with expanded tracking to ensure immediate recognizability at a glance.

## Layout & Spacing

The system utilizes an operational desktop-first fluid grid based on a 4px/8px incremental cadence.

### Canvas Grid Model
- **Workspaces:** 12-column dynamic grid with a minimum viewport width support of 360px up to 2560px ultra-wide command surfaces.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Multi-pane panels split between legal document viewer, agent intelligence console, and contextual inspector.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px canvas margins. Secondary agent inspectors dock into slide-over sheets.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px canvas margins. Single primary focus stream with floating state triggers.

### Component Spacing Discipline
Internal component paddings must maintain structural stability. Never mix ad-hoc margins; utilize `space-sm` for inline token grouping, `space-md` for internal panel padding, and `space-xl` for operational section breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via atmospheric luminosity, translucent boundary refraction, and optical layering rather than traditional drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#080B12`. Textured with a subtle SVG dot matrix (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)) spaced at 24px intervals to reinforce mathematical rigor.
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Shells):** `#101624` with a 1px perimeter outline of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Inspect Cards & Active Inspectors):** `#151D2D` with backdrop filter blur (16px) and a primary border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover or active states introduce a subtle directional glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(67, 217, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Surface `#151D2D` overlaid onto a `rgba(8, 11, 18, 0.8)` blurred backdrop (24px blur). Edge boundaries are enhanced with a dual gradient border transitioning from `rgba(67, 217, 255, 0.2)` at the top edge to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.05)` at the bottom edge.

### Atmosphere & Lighting
- **Radial Blooms:** Local agent cards utilize an internal ambient spotlight (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08), transparent 70%)`).
- **Data Glow:** Focus indicators emit a sharp 1px neon line accompanied by a diffused 8px blur at 15% opacity, preserving dark-field clarity without visual bleeding.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a crisp, technical shape profile (Level 1: Soft). By keeping radii restrained (4px baseline, 8px for containers, 12px for primary structural modals), components maintain the precision of specialized instrumentation.

### Shape Metrics
- **Interactive Micro-Components (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** 4px (`rounded-sm`). Communicates mechanical control and density.
- **Cards, Panels, and Floating Inspectors:** 8px (`rounded-lg`). Softens the perimeter while maintaining crisp vertical alignment with code and data grids.
- **Large Modals & Canvas Windows:** 12px (`rounded-xl`).
- **Status Indicators & Micro Tags:** Fully rounded pill-shapes are explicitly banned, except for circular 6px agent pulse nodes. All badges must retain structural 4px corners to avoid consumer-app associations.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary (Agent Action):** Background in Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) with white text (`#F4F7FF`). Subtle inset box-shadow: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover, elevates with a luminous cyan-tinted edge highlight.
- **Secondary (Analytical/Outline):** Surface `#101624`, border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#F4F7FF`. Hover triggers border transition to `rgba(67, 217, 255, 0.3)` and background `#151D2D`.
- **Destructive/Risk Flag:** Dark crimson ground with an amber-red hairline border (`rgba(255, 105, 105, 0.2)`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: Surface `#101624`.
- Borders: 1px uniform `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Active/Focused: Hairline border shifts to `#43D9FF` with an exterior glow of `0 0 0 1px rgba(67, 217, 255, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#9BA8BD` at 50% opacity. 
- Integrated telemetry: Search fields display keyboard shortcut hints (`⌘K`) in JetBrains Mono (`label-sm`).

### Intelligence Badges & Metadata Chips
- Non-pill, rectangular tags with 4px border radius.
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Text: Rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-sm`).
- Dynamic Agent Tags include a leading 6px glowing indicator dot:
  - Green (`#5DE2B1`): Clause validated / Consensus reached.
  - Cyan (`#43D9FF`): Agent parsing / Real-time calculation.
  - Amber (`#FFBE69`): Ambiguity detected / Review required.

### Cards & Contract Panels
- Multi-layered panels: Top bar containing contract hash and clause status separated by a 1px border line (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Interior padding: Strict 16px or 24px increments.
- Glass variant: `#151D2D` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and 1px border `rgba(67, 217, 255, 0.15)` for active AI agents actively streaming diff analyses.

### Checkboxes & Binary Selectors
- Crisp 4px squared targets (`16x16px`).
- Inactive: Border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, background transparent.
- Checked: Background `#8B5CF6`, border-color `#8B5CF6`, checkmark icon pure white, minimal 0.5px offset.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Agent Orchestration Rail:** A persistent edge component depicting concurrent autonomous legal agents with real-time status pulses, token consumption velocity, and sub-second reasoning state indicators.
- **Clause Redline Inspector:** Dual-pane micro-diff engine with token-level highlighting utilizing semi-transparent violet overlays for AI proposals and amber strikes for deleted liabilities.
