SECTION 1 — SCOPE OF SERVICES
-------------------------------

1.1  Engagement. Company engages Contractor, and Contractor agrees to provide,
     the design and development services described in the Statement of Work
     attached as Exhibit A (the "Services").

1.2  Statement of Work. The initial Statement of Work is incorporated into
     this Agreement. Additional Statements of Work may be executed by the
     Parties from time to time and will be governed by this Agreement.

1.3  Performance Standard. Contractor will perform the Services in a
     professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted
     industry standards.

SECTION 2 — TERM
------------------

2.1  Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until
     December 31, 2027, unless earlier terminated in accordance with
     Section 9 (the "Term").

2.2  No Auto-Renewal. This Agreement does not automatically renew. Any
     extension must be agreed in writing by both Parties.

SECTION 3 — COMPENSATION
--------------------------

3.1  Fees. Company will pay Contractor the fees set forth in each Statement
     of Work. For the initial Statement of Work (Exhibit A), the total fixed
     fee is Sixty-Five Thousand U.S. Dollars ($65,000.00), payable in
     milestones as follows:

       Milestone    Description                        Amount      Due
       ----------   --------------------------------   ---------   ---------
       M1           Project kickoff and discovery       $10,000    Upon execution
       M2           Design mockups delivery             $15,000    Upon acceptance
       M3           Front-end development delivery      $20,000    Upon acceptance
       M4           Integration and testing delivery    $12,000    Upon acceptance
       M5           Final delivery and launch           $8,000     Upon acceptance

3.2  Invoicing. Contractor will submit an invoice for each milestone within
     five (5) business days after Company accepts the milestone deliverable
     (or the milestone is deemed accepted under Section 4.3). Company will
     pay each invoice within twenty (20) business days of receipt.

3.3  Late Payment. If Company fails to pay any undisputed invoice within the
     payment period, Contractor may charge interest at the rate of one percent
     (1.0%) per month on the overdue amount from the due date until paid in
     full.

3.4  Expenses. Company will reimburse Contractor for reasonable, pre-approved
     out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing the Services. Contractor
     must submit expense reports with receipts within thirty (30) days of
     incurring the expense.

SECTION 4 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE
-----------------------------------------

4.1  Delivery. Contractor will deliver each milestone deliverable by the date
     specified in the Statement of Work. If a delivery date falls on a
     weekend or holiday observed by Company (as listed in the holiday
     schedule provided by Company), the delivery date will be the next
     business day.

4.2  Review Period. Company will have ten (10) business days after receipt of
     each milestone deliverable to review the deliverable and either accept
     it or provide Contractor with a written list of deficiencies (a
     "Rejection Notice"). Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and
     holidays observed by Company as listed in the holiday schedule provided
     by Company (data/holidays.csv in this bundle).

4.3  Deemed Acceptance. If Company does not deliver a Rejection Notice within
     the ten (10) business day review period specified in Section 4.2, the
     milestone deliverable will be deemed accepted.

4.4  Corrections. If Company delivers a Rejection Notice, Contractor will
     correct the identified deficiencies within five (5) business days and
     re-deliver the corrected deliverable. Company will then have five (5)
     business days to review the corrected deliverable.

4.5  Dispute. If the Parties cannot agree on whether a deliverable meets the
     requirements in the Statement of Work after two rounds of correction,
     either Party may escalate the matter to the senior executives
     designated in Section 10.

SECTION 5 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
-----------------------------------

5.1  Work Product. All deliverables, work product, and materials created by
     Contractor in performing the Services ("Work Product") are works made
     for hire to the extent permitted by Applicable Laws. To the extent any
     Work Product is not a work made for hire, Contractor hereby assigns to
     Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product,
     including all intellectual property rights.

5.2  Pre-Existing Materials. Contractor retains ownership of any tools,
     frameworks, libraries, or other materials that Contractor developed
     before or independently of this Agreement ("Pre-Existing Materials").
     To the extent Contractor incorporates Pre-Existing Materials into the
     Work Product, Contractor grants Company a non-exclusive, perpetual,
     irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, modify, and distribute the
     Pre-Existing Materials as part of the Work Product.

5.3  Open-Source Software. Contractor will not incorporate any open-source
     software into the Work Product without Company's prior written approval.
     Contractor will identify all proposed open-source components, their
     licenses, and any obligations they impose before incorporating them.

SECTION 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY
-----------------------------

6.1  Definition. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information
     disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement
     that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would
     understand to be confidential.

6.2  Obligations. Each Party will: (a) use the other Party's Confidential
     Information only for the purposes of this Agreement; (b) protect the
     other Party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree
     of care it uses for its own confidential information, but no less than
     reasonable care; and (c) not disclose the other Party's Confidential
     Information to any third party except as necessary to perform the
     Services, provided such third party is bound by confidentiality
     obligations at least as protective as those in this Section 6.

6.3  Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that:
     (a) is or becomes publicly available without fault of the receiving
     Party; (b) the receiving Party already knew without obligation of
     confidentiality; (c) the receiving Party receives from a third party
     without obligation of confidentiality; or (d) the receiving Party
     independently developed without reference to the disclosing Party's
     Confidential Information.

SECTION 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
--------------------------------------------

7.1  Mutual. Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it has the legal
     power and authority to enter into this Agreement; and (b) it will
     comply with all Applicable Laws in performing its obligations.

7.2  Contractor. Contractor represents and warrants that: (a) it has the
     skills, experience, and qualifications necessary to perform the
     Services; (b) the Work Product will be original and will not infringe
     any third party's intellectual property rights; and (c) the Work
     Product will conform to the specifications in the Statement of Work.

SECTION 8 — INDEMNIFICATION
-----------------------------

8.1  By Contractor. Contractor will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless
     Company from and against any claims, damages, losses, and expenses
     (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: (a) Contractor's
     breach of Section 7.2; (b) any claim that the Work Product infringes
     a third party's intellectual property rights; or (c) Contractor's
     gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services.

8.2  By Company. Company will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless
     Contractor from and against any claims, damages, losses, and expenses
     (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Company's gross
     negligence or willful misconduct.

SECTION 9 — TERMINATION
-------------------------

9.1  Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement
     upon thirty (30) calendar days' written notice to the other Party.

9.2  Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this Agreement
     immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (a) materially
     breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen
     (15) calendar days after receiving written notice; or (b) becomes
     insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

9.3  Effect of Termination. Upon termination: (a) Company will pay
     Contractor for all Services performed and accepted (or deemed accepted)
     through the date of termination; (b) Contractor will deliver to
     Company all Work Product completed or in progress; and (c) each
     Party will return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information.

10.7 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other

10.8 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or

Project:       Route Optimizer Dashboard Redesign
Duration:      October 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027
Project Lead:  Elena Vasquez (Contractor) / Dana Kowalski (Company)

Objective:     Redesign the Route Optimizer customer-facing dashboard to
               improve usability, add real-time fleet visualization, and
               integrate a new analytics reporting module.

Milestones:

  M1 — Project Kickoff and Discovery
    Deliverables:  Kickoff meeting notes, requirements document, project plan
    Due Date:      October 15, 2026
    Fee:           $10,000

  M2 — Design Mockups
    Deliverables:  High-fidelity mockups for dashboard, fleet view, analytics
                   module, and mobile-responsive layouts (Figma files)
    Due Date:      November 30, 2026
    Fee:           $15,000

  M3 — Front-End Development
    Deliverables:  Functional front-end implementation (React), component
                   library, unit tests with ≥80% code coverage
    Due Date:      January 31, 2027
    Fee:           $20,000

  M4 — Integration and Testing
    Deliverables:  API integration with Route Optimizer backend, end-to-end
                   tests, performance benchmarks, accessibility audit (WCAG
                   2.1 AA compliance)
    Due Date:      February 28, 2027
    Fee:           $12,000

  M5 — Final Delivery and Launch
    Deliverables:  Production deployment, documentation, 2-hour training
                   session for Quillhub support staff, 30-day post-launch
                   defect support
    Due Date:      March 31, 2027
    Fee:           $8,000

Acceptance Criteria:
  - All deliverables must conform to the specifications in this Statement
    of Work.
  - Code deliverables must pass all automated tests and code review by
    Company's engineering team.
  - Design deliverables must be approved by Company's product team.
  - Acceptance and deemed-acceptance are governed by Section 4 of this
    Agreement.
  - Business days for the review periods in Section 4 exclude weekends and
    holidays listed in the holiday schedule provided by Company.
