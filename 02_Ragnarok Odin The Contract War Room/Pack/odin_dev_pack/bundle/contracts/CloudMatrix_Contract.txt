================================================================================
                    CLOUD SERVICES AGREEMENT
================================================================================
This Cloud Services Agreement is entered into by and between CloudMatrix Inc. (Provider)
and Global Logistics Corp. (Customer) effective as of March 1, 2026.

SECTION 1: TERM AND DURATION
The Initial Term shall be 24 months from the Effective Date.

SECTION 2: RENEWAL
This Agreement automatically renews for successive 12-month periods unless either
party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 calendar days prior to expiration.

SECTION 3: FEES AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Customer shall pay a recurring monthly fee of $25,000 USD. Invoices shall be paid
within net 30 days of invoice date.

SECTION 4: OBLIGATIONS
Provider shall maintain system availability and daily encrypted backups of Customer data.
Customer shall maintain administrative account credentials with multi-factor authentication.

SECTION 5: PENALTIES AND SERVICE CREDITS
If monthly availability falls below 99.5%, Customer is entitled to a 15% service credit
against the monthly fee.

SECTION 6: COMPLIANCE AND SECURITY
Each party warrants compliance with applicable laws, including GDPR data protection
regulations and SOC 2 Type II security measures.

SECTION 7: TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon 30 days written notice if
the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure within 30 days.
