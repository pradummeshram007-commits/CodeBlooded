Section A — Cloud Service Description
--------------------------------------
The Cloud Service is StratoNode CloudCore, a managed cloud infrastructure
platform providing compute, storage, network, and monitoring services. Quillhub
will use StratoNode CloudCore to host and operate its Route Optimizer
application.

Section B — Subscription Period and Renewal
--------------------------------------------
  Effective Date:            August 1, 2026
  Initial Subscription Period: 24 months (ending July 31, 2028)
  Renewal Period:            successive 12-month periods
  Non-Renewal Notice Period: 90 days before the end of the then-current
                             Subscription Period

  The Agreement will automatically renew for successive Renewal Periods of
  12 months each, unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal
  to the other party's Notice Address at least 90 days before the end of the
  then-current Subscription Period, in accordance with Section 5.1 of the
  Standard Terms.

Section C — Fees and Payment
-----------------------------
  Monthly Fee:     $8,000.00 (Eight Thousand U.S. Dollars)
  Payment Process: Invoicing. StratoNode will issue invoices on the first
                   business day of each calendar month for that month's Fee.
  Payment Period:  30 days from invoice date.
  Currency:        U.S. Dollars

  Late Payment:    In accordance with Section 4.4 of the Standard Terms,
                   overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

Section D — Technical Support
------------------------------
  Support Tier:     Business Support
  Support Hours:    Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM Pacific Time
                    (excluding StratoNode-observed holidays)
  Response Targets:
    - Severity 1 (service down):         1 hour
    - Severity 2 (significant impact):   4 hours
    - Severity 3 (minor impact):         1 business day
    - Severity 4 (general inquiry):      2 business days

  StratoNode does not guarantee resolution times. Response targets are
  commercially reasonable objectives, not contractual commitments.

Section E — Service Level Agreement
------------------------------------
  The Cloud Service is subject to the Service Level Agreement attached hereto

  Agreement by reference. In the event of a conflict between this Cover Page
  and the SLA, the SLA controls with respect to uptime commitments and service
  credits.

Section F — Data and Security
------------------------------
  Data Location:      United States (us-west-2 and us-east-1 regions)
  Encryption at Rest: AES-256
  Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.2 or higher
  Backup Frequency:   Daily automated snapshots, retained for 30 days

  Security Incident Notification: In accordance with Section 3.3 of the
  Standard Terms, StratoNode will notify Quillhub within 72 hours of becoming
  aware of a Security Incident.

Section I — Additional Terms
------------------------------

I.1  Incident Notification to Customer. If StratoNode experiences an
     unplanned service disruption affecting the Cloud Service, StratoNode
     will use commercially reasonable efforts to notify Quillhub's
     Operations contact (ops@quillhub.io) within four (4) hours of becoming
     aware of the disruption. This notification obligation is in addition to
     the Security Incident notification in Section 3.3 of the Standard Terms.

I.2  Migration Assistance. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement,
     StratoNode will provide reasonable migration assistance to Quillhub for
     a period of up to 90 days following the effective date of termination,
     at StratoNode's then-current professional services rates.

I.3  Subprocessors. StratoNode may use subprocessors to deliver the Cloud
     Service. StratoNode will maintain a list of subprocessors and notify
     Quillhub of any changes at least 30 days in advance.
