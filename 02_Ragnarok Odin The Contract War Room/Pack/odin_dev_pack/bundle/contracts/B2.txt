================================================================================
           DATA PROCESSING AND SECURITY ADDENDUM TO THE
         CLOUD SERVICE AGREEMENT (QH-HARBOR-2026-001)
================================================================================

                    DOCUMENT ID: QH-HARBOR-2026-DPA

This Data Processing and Security Addendum ("Addendum") supplements the Cloud
Service Agreement between Quillhub Inc. ("Provider") and Harbor Retail Group
LLC ("Customer") dated September 15, 2026 (Contract ID: QH-HARBOR-2026-001,
the "Agreement"). This Addendum is incorporated into and forms part of the
Agreement.

Capitalized terms not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the
Agreement or the Standard Terms.

In the event of a conflict between this Addendum and the Agreement (including
the Standard Terms), this Addendum controls with respect to data processing,
security, and breach notification obligations.

================================================================================

SECTION 1 — SCOPE AND ROLES
-----------------------------

1.1  This Addendum applies to the processing of Personal Data that Harbor
     Retail submits to or makes accessible through the Platform.

1.2  For the purposes of Applicable Data Protection Laws:
     (a) Harbor Retail is the data controller (or equivalent role);
     (b) Quillhub is the data processor (or equivalent role).

1.3  Quillhub will process Personal Data only on documented instructions
     from Harbor Retail, including as set forth in this Addendum and the
     Agreement, unless required to do so by Applicable Laws. In such a case,
     Quillhub will inform Harbor Retail of that legal requirement before
     processing, unless Applicable Laws prohibit such notice.

SECTION 2 — DATA SECURITY
---------------------------

2.1  Security Measures. Quillhub will implement and maintain appropriate
     technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against
     unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss,
     destruction, damage, theft, or disclosure. These measures include, at a
     minimum:

     (a) Encryption of Personal Data at rest using AES-256 and in transit
         using TLS 1.2 or higher;
     (b) Access controls that limit access to Personal Data to authorized
         personnel on a need-to-know basis;
     (c) Regular testing and evaluation of the effectiveness of security
         measures;
     (d) Employee security training at least annually;
     (e) Secure disposal of media containing Personal Data.

2.2  Access Logging. Quillhub will maintain access logs for systems that
     process Personal Data and will retain such logs for at least twelve (12)
     months.

2.3  Vulnerability Management. Quillhub will perform vulnerability scans of
     its systems at least quarterly and will remediate critical
     vulnerabilities within thirty (30) days of discovery.

SECTION 3 — DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
--------------------------------------

3.1  Notification Timing. If Quillhub becomes aware of a Data Breach (as
     defined in Section 5.1 of the Agreement), Quillhub will notify Harbor
     Retail within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of the Data
     Breach. For the purposes of this Addendum, Quillhub "becomes aware" of
     a Data Breach at the point at which Quillhub has a reasonable degree of
     certainty that a security incident has resulted in unauthorized access
     to Personal Data.

3.2  Notification Contents. The notification will include, to the extent
     available: (a) the nature of the Data Breach; (b) the categories and
     approximate number of individuals and records affected; (c) the contact
     details of Quillhub's privacy officer or other contact; (d) the likely
     consequences; (e) the measures taken or proposed to address the breach
     and mitigate its effects.

3.3  Cooperation. Quillhub will cooperate with Harbor Retail in investigating
     and remediating the Data Breach and will provide reasonable assistance
     in fulfilling Harbor Retail's obligations to notify individuals and
     regulatory authorities, at Quillhub's expense if the Data Breach was
     caused by Quillhub's failure to comply with this Addendum.

3.4  Record Keeping. Quillhub will maintain a record of all Data Breaches,
     including the facts relating to the breach, its effects, and the
     remedial action taken.

SECTION 4 — SUBPROCESSORS
---------------------------

4.1  Authorization. Harbor Retail grants Quillhub general authorization to
     engage subprocessors to process Personal Data, subject to the
     requirements in this Section 4.

4.2  Current Subprocessors. As of the Effective Date, the following
     subprocessors are authorized:

       Subprocessor          Service                   Location
       -------------------   -----------------------   ---------------
       StratoNode Inc.       Cloud infrastructure      United States
       SendGrid Inc.         Transactional email       United States
       Logfire Analytics     Usage analytics            United States

4.3  New Subprocessors. Quillhub will notify Harbor Retail of any new
     subprocessor at least thirty (30) days before the new subprocessor
     begins processing Personal Data. The notification will include the
     subprocessor's name, the processing activities, and the location of
     processing.

4.4  Objection. Harbor Retail may object to a new subprocessor in writing
     within fifteen (15) days of receiving notice. If Harbor Retail objects
     on reasonable data-protection grounds and Quillhub cannot address the
     objection within thirty (30) days, Harbor Retail may terminate this
     Addendum and the Agreement upon written notice.

SECTION 5 — DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
-----------------------------------------

5.1  Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, Quillhub will, at
     Harbor Retail's election, delete or return all Personal Data within
     sixty (60) days. Quillhub will certify in writing that deletion has
     been completed.

5.2  Quillhub may retain Personal Data to the extent required by Applicable
     Laws, provided that Quillhub will (a) limit such retention to the
     minimum necessary; (b) continue to protect the retained data in
     accordance with this Addendum; and (c) delete the retained data when
     the legal obligation has been fulfilled.

SECTION 6 — AUDITS
--------------------

6.1  Quillhub will make available to Harbor Retail, upon reasonable request
     and at least once per year, information necessary to demonstrate
     compliance with this Addendum. This may include third-party audit
     reports (e.g., SOC 2 Type II), security certifications, or summaries
     of internal assessments.

6.2  Harbor Retail may, upon sixty (60) days' written notice, conduct or
     commission an audit of Quillhub's data-processing activities, limited
     to once per twelve-month period unless a Data Breach has occurred.
     Quillhub will cooperate with the audit and provide reasonable access
     to relevant facilities, systems, and personnel.

SECTION 7 — INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
--------------------------------------

7.1  Quillhub will not transfer Personal Data outside the United States
     without Harbor Retail's prior written consent.

7.2  If a transfer outside the United States is authorized, Quillhub will
     ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place as required by
     Applicable Data Protection Laws, including standard contractual clauses
     or other approved transfer mechanisms.

SECTION 8 — LIABILITY
-----------------------

8.1  Quillhub's liability for breaches of this Addendum will be subject to
     the limitation of liability provisions in the Agreement, except that
     Data Breach obligations under Section 3 of this Addendum are Unlimited
     Claims as specified in Section J of the Cover Page.
