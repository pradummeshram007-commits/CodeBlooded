================================================================================
                 AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
================================================================================

                    DOCUMENT ID: QH-STRATO-2026-AMD1

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") amends the Service Level Agreement dated
August 1, 2026, Document ID QH-STRATO-2026-SLA (the "SLA"), which is
incorporated by reference into the Cloud Service Agreement between StratoNode
Inc. ("Provider") and Quillhub Inc. ("Customer") dated August 1, 2026
(Contract ID: QH-STRATO-2026-001, the "Agreement").

Capitalized terms not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in
the SLA, the Agreement, or the Standard Terms.

Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, the SLA remains in full force
and effect. In the event of a conflict between this Amendment and the SLA,
this Amendment controls.

================================================================================

AMENDMENT EFFECTIVE DATE:  January 1, 2027

================================================================================

SECTION 1 — MODIFICATION TO CREDIT REQUEST PROCEDURE
------------------------------------------------------

Section 3.2 of the SLA is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the
following:

  "3.2  Credit Request Procedure. To receive a Service Credit, Customer must
        submit a written request to Provider's Notice Address within fifteen
        (15) days after the end of the calendar month in which the Downtime
        occurred. The request must include: (a) the specific dates and times
        of each Downtime incident; (b) a description of how the Cloud Service
        was affected; (c) evidence from Customer's own monitoring systems or
        Provider's status page; and (d) the calculated Monthly Uptime
        Percentage. Provider will review the request and respond within ten
        (10) business days. If Provider confirms that the Uptime Target was
        not met, Provider will apply the Service Credit to Customer's next
        invoice."

SECTION 2 — MODIFICATION TO SERVICE CREDIT CALCULATION
-------------------------------------------------------

Section 3.1 of the SLA is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the
following:

  "3.1  If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the Uptime Target in any
        calendar month, Customer will be eligible for a Service Credit as
        specified in the table below:

          Monthly Uptime Percentage          Service Credit
          ----------------------------       -------------------------
          99.0% to < 99.5%                   10% of Monthly Fee
          95.0% to < 99.0%                   25% of Monthly Fee
          Below 95.0%                        50% of Monthly Fee

        'Monthly Fee' for the purpose of this SLA means the monthly Fee
        payable under the Agreement, currently Eight Thousand U.S. Dollars
        ($8,000.00).

        Additionally, if Downtime in a single incident exceeds sixty (60)
        consecutive minutes, Customer will receive an additional Service
        Credit equal to five percent (5%) of the Monthly Fee for that month,
        in addition to the credit specified in the table above. This
        additional credit is subject to the maximum credit in Section 3.3."

SECTION 3 — MODIFICATION TO NOTICE WINDOW
-------------------------------------------

Section B of the Cover Page (Contract ID: QH-STRATO-2026-001) is amended as
follows:

  The Non-Renewal Notice Period is changed from ninety (90) days to sixty
  (60) days before the end of the then-current Subscription Period.

  For clarity, the amended non-renewal provision reads: "The Agreement will
  automatically renew for successive Renewal Periods of 12 months each,
  unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other
  party's Notice Address at least 60 days before the end of the then-current
  Subscription Period, in accordance with Section 5.1 of the Standard Terms."

All other terms and conditions of the SLA and the Agreement remain unchanged
and in full force and effect.
