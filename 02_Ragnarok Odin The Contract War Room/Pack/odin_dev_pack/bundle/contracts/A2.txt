================================================================================
              STRATONODE CLOUDCORE — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
================================================================================

                    DOCUMENT ID: QH-STRATO-2026-SLA

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") is incorporated by reference into the
Cloud Service Agreement between StratoNode Inc. ("Provider") and Quillhub Inc.
("Customer") dated August 1, 2026 (Contract ID: QH-STRATO-2026-001, the
"Agreement"). Capitalized terms not defined in this SLA have the meanings
given in the Agreement or the Standard Terms.

In the event of a conflict between this SLA and the Agreement, this SLA
controls with respect to uptime commitments and service credits.

================================================================================

SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS
------------------------

1.1  "Monthly Uptime Percentage" means the total number of minutes in a
     calendar month minus the number of Downtime Minutes, divided by the total
     number of minutes in that calendar month, expressed as a percentage.

1.2  "Downtime Minutes" means the total number of minutes during which the
     Cloud Service is unavailable, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and any
     unavailability caused by circumstances described in Section 5.

1.3  "Scheduled Maintenance" means maintenance that Provider has notified
     Customer about at least 48 hours in advance via email to the Customer's
     Operations contact or through the Provider's status page. Scheduled
     Maintenance windows will not exceed four (4) hours per occurrence and
     will not be scheduled more than twice per calendar month.

1.4  "Service Credit" means a credit against future Fees, calculated as
     described in Section 3.

SECTION 2 — UPTIME COMMITMENT
-------------------------------

2.1  Provider will make the Cloud Service available at least ninety-nine
     and one-half percent (99.5%) of the minutes in each calendar month, not
     counting Scheduled Maintenance (the "Uptime Target").

SECTION 3 — SERVICE CREDITS
-----------------------------

3.1  If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the Uptime Target in any
     calendar month, Customer will be eligible for a Service Credit as
     specified in the table below:

       Monthly Uptime Percentage          Service Credit
       ----------------------------       -------------------------
       99.0% to < 99.5%                   10% of Monthly Fee
       95.0% to < 99.0%                   25% of Monthly Fee
       Below 95.0%                        50% of Monthly Fee

     "Monthly Fee" for the purpose of this SLA means the monthly Fee payable
     under the Agreement, currently Eight Thousand U.S. Dollars ($8,000.00).

3.2  Credit Request Procedure. To receive a Service Credit, Customer must
     submit a written request to Provider's Notice Address within thirty (30)
     days after the end of the calendar month in which the Downtime occurred.
     The request must include: (a) the dates and times of the Downtime;
     (b) a description of how the Cloud Service was affected; and (c) any
     supporting evidence such as error logs or screenshots. Provider will
     review the request and, if it confirms that the Uptime Target was not
     met, will apply the Service Credit to Customer's next invoice.

3.3  Maximum Credit. The total Service Credits for any single calendar month
     will not exceed fifty percent (50%) of the Monthly Fee for that month.

3.4  Sole Remedy. Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy
     for Provider's failure to meet the Uptime Target, except as otherwise
     provided in the Agreement.

SECTION 4 — REPORTING
----------------------

4.1  Provider will make Monthly Uptime Percentage data available to Customer
     through the Provider's online dashboard or upon written request.

4.2  Provider will maintain service-availability logs for at least twelve
     (12) months and will make them available to Customer upon reasonable
     request for the purpose of verifying Service Credits.

SECTION 5 — EXCLUSIONS
------------------------

The following are excluded from the calculation of Downtime Minutes:

5.1  Scheduled Maintenance, as defined in Section 1.3.

5.2  Downtime caused by Customer's equipment, software, network connections,
     or other infrastructure not provided by Provider.

5.3  Downtime caused by Customer's use of the Cloud Service in a manner not
     authorized by the Agreement or the Documentation.

5.4  Downtime caused by a Force Majeure Event as defined in the Standard Terms.

5.5  Downtime resulting from actions or inactions of third parties outside
     Provider's reasonable control, including internet service providers,
     DNS providers, and certificate authorities.

SECTION 6 — TERMINATION FOR PERSISTENT FAILURES
-------------------------------------------------

6.1  If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the Uptime Target in any
     two (2) consecutive calendar months, Customer may, in addition to any
     Service Credits, terminate the Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written
     notice to Provider. In such event, Provider will refund to Customer a
     prorated portion of any prepaid Fees for the remainder of the
     Subscription Period.

================================================================================

This SLA is effective as of the Effective Date of the Agreement and will
remain in effect for the duration of the Agreement, including any renewal
periods.
