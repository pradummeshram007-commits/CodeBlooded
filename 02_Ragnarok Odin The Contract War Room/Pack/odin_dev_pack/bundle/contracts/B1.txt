Section A — Cloud Service Description
--------------------------------------
The Cloud Service is Quillhub Route Optimizer Enterprise, a cloud-based
logistics-optimization platform providing real-time route planning, fleet
tracking, delivery-window management, and API integration services (the
"Platform").

Section B — Subscription Period and Renewal
--------------------------------------------
  Effective Date:              September 15, 2026
  Initial Subscription Period: 36 months (ending September 14, 2029)
  Renewal Period:              successive 24-month periods
  Non-Renewal Notice Period:   180 days before the end of the then-current
                               Subscription Period

  The Agreement will automatically renew for successive Renewal Periods of
  24 months each, unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal
  to the other party's Notice Address at least 180 days before the end of the
  then-current Subscription Period, in accordance with Section 5.1 of the
  Standard Terms.

Section C — Fees and Payment
-----------------------------
  Monthly Fee:     $40,000.00 (Forty Thousand U.S. Dollars)
  Payment Process: Invoicing. Quillhub will issue invoices on the first
                   business day of each calendar month for that month's Fee.
  Payment Period:  45 days from invoice date.
  Currency:        U.S. Dollars

  Annual Fee Adjustment: Beginning with the second year of the Subscription
  Period, the Monthly Fee will increase by three percent (3%) on each
  anniversary of the Effective Date. For clarity, the Monthly Fee for the
  period beginning September 15, 2027 will be $41,200.00.

Section D — Service Level
--------------------------
  §3.1  Uptime Commitment. Quillhub will make the Platform available at least
        ninety-nine and nine-tenths percent (99.9%) of the minutes in each
        calendar month, not counting Scheduled Maintenance (the "Harbor Uptime
        Target"). "Scheduled Maintenance" means maintenance that Quillhub has
        notified Harbor Retail about at least 72 hours in advance via email to
        the Customer's contact. Scheduled Maintenance windows will not exceed
        two (2) hours per occurrence and will not be scheduled more than once
        per calendar month.

  §3.2  Uptime Measurement. "Monthly Uptime Percentage" means the total
        number of minutes in a calendar month minus the number of Downtime
        Minutes, divided by the total number of minutes in that calendar
        month, expressed as a percentage. "Downtime Minutes" means the total
        number of minutes during which the Platform is unavailable, excluding
        Scheduled Maintenance.

  §3.3  Service Credits. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the
        Harbor Uptime Target in any calendar month, Quillhub will credit
        Harbor Retail fifteen percent (15%) of the Monthly Fee for that month
        against Harbor Retail's next invoice.

  §3.4  Credit Cap. The total service credits for any single calendar month
        will not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the Monthly Fee for that month.

  §3.5  Credit Request. Harbor Retail must request a service credit in writing
        within forty-five (45) days after the end of the calendar month in
        which the Downtime occurred.

Section E — Incident Response
------------------------------
  §4.1  Incident Notification. If Quillhub becomes aware of any unplanned
        service disruption affecting the Platform, Quillhub will notify Harbor
        Retail's contact (nora.chen@harborretail.com) within four (4) hours
        of becoming aware of the disruption.

  §4.2  Incident Reports. For any unplanned Downtime exceeding thirty (30)
        minutes, Quillhub will provide a written root-cause analysis to
        Harbor Retail within five (5) business days after service is restored.

Section F — Data Protection
-----------------------------
  §5.1  Data Breach Notification. If Quillhub becomes aware of any actual or
        reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or unauthorized
        acquisition, disclosure, use, or loss of, Harbor Retail's data
        ("Data Breach"), Quillhub will notify Harbor Retail's General Counsel
        within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of the Data Breach.
        This obligation is in addition to and does not replace the Security
        Incident notification obligation in Section 3.3 of the Standard Terms.

  §5.2  Breach Notification Contents. The Data Breach notification must
        include: (a) a description of the nature of the breach; (b) the
        categories and approximate number of records affected; (c) the likely
        consequences; and (d) the measures taken or proposed to address the
        breach.

Section G — Exclusivity
-------------------------
  §6.1  Route Optimizer Exclusivity. During the Subscription Period, Quillhub
        grants Harbor Retail the exclusive right to use the Route Optimizer
        Enterprise platform within the Pacific Northwest retail grocery
        sector (defined as retail grocery operations headquartered in the
        states of Washington and Oregon). Quillhub will not license Route
        Optimizer Enterprise to any other retail grocery company headquartered
        in Washington or Oregon during the Subscription Period without Harbor
        Retail's prior written consent.

Section H — Insurance Requirement
-----------------------------------
  §7.1  Provider Insurance. Quillhub will maintain, at its own expense and
        throughout the Subscription Period, the following insurance coverage:
        (a) Commercial General Liability insurance with limits of at least
        $2,000,000 per occurrence; and (b) Technology Errors & Omissions and
        Cyber Liability insurance with limits of at least $3,000,000 per
        claim. Quillhub will provide certificates of insurance to Harbor
        Retail upon request within ten (10) business days.

  §7.2  Lapse Notification. If any required insurance policy is cancelled,
        materially reduced, or not renewed, Quillhub will notify Harbor
        Retail in writing within five (5) business days. A lapse in required
        insurance coverage constitutes a material breach of this Agreement.

Section I — Termination
-------------------------
  §8.1  Termination for Persistent SLA Failures. If the Monthly Uptime
        Percentage falls below the Harbor Uptime Target in any two (2)
        consecutive calendar months, Harbor Retail may terminate this
        Agreement upon written notice to Quillhub. In such event, Quillhub
        will refund to Harbor Retail a prorated portion of any prepaid Fees
        for the remainder of the Subscription Period.

  §8.2  Termination for Insurance Lapse. If Quillhub fails to maintain the
        insurance required by Section 7 and does not cure the lapse within
        fifteen (15) days after written notice from Harbor Retail, Harbor
        Retail may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice.

Section J — Liability

                       twelve (12) months immediately preceding the claim.
  Unlimited Claims:    Data Breach obligations under Section 5;
                       Customer's breach of Section 2.1 of the Standard Terms
                       (Restrictions on Customer).
