Section A — Cloud Service Description
--------------------------------------
The Cloud Service is Quillhub Route Optimizer Pro, a cloud-based logistics-
optimization platform providing real-time route planning, fleet tracking,
delivery-window management, and standard API access (the "Platform").

Section B — Subscription Period and Renewal
--------------------------------------------
  Effective Date:              [TO BE INSERTED UPON GO-LIVE]
  Initial Subscription Period: 24 months from the Effective Date
  Renewal Period:              successive 12-month periods
  Non-Renewal Notice Period:   90 days before the end of the then-current
                               Subscription Period

  The Agreement will automatically renew for successive Renewal Periods of
  12 months each, unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal
  to the other party's Notice Address at least 90 days before the end of the
  then-current Subscription Period, in accordance with Section 5.1 of the
  Standard Terms.

  "Written notice" means a notice delivered in accordance with Section 12.4
  of the Standard Terms. For the avoidance of doubt, informal communications
  (including but not limited to phone calls, text messages, and emails sent
  to addresses other than the Notice Address) do not constitute written
  notice for the purposes of this Agreement.

Section D — Service Level
--------------------------
  §3.1  Uptime Commitment. Quillhub will make the Platform available at least
        ninety-nine and five-tenths percent (99.5%) of the minutes in each
        calendar month, not counting Scheduled Maintenance (the "Vantage
        Uptime Target"). "Scheduled Maintenance" means maintenance that
        Quillhub has notified Vantage Logistics about at least 48 hours in
        advance via email to the Customer's contact.

  §3.2  Service Credits. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the
        Vantage Uptime Target in any calendar month, Quillhub will credit
        Vantage Logistics ten percent (10%) of the Monthly Fee for that month
        against Vantage Logistics' next invoice.

  §3.3  Credit Request. Vantage Logistics must request a service credit in
        writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the calendar month
        in which the Downtime occurred.

Section E — Exclusivity
-------------------------
  §4.1  Route Optimizer Exclusivity. During the Subscription Period, Quillhub
        grants Vantage Logistics the exclusive right to use the Route
        Optimizer platform (including all tiers: Standard, Pro, and
        Enterprise) within the Pacific Northwest third-party logistics sector
        (defined as third-party logistics companies headquartered in the
        states of Washington and Oregon that provide warehousing and last-mile
        delivery services). Quillhub will not license any tier of Route
        Optimizer to any other third-party logistics company headquartered in
        Washington or Oregon that provides warehousing and last-mile delivery
        services during the Subscription Period without Vantage Logistics'
        prior written consent.

Section H — Additional Terms
------------------------------
  H.1  Customer Data Ownership. For the avoidance of doubt, all route data,
       delivery data, fleet telemetry, and optimization outputs generated
       through Vantage Logistics' use of the Platform ("Vantage Operations
       Data") are Customer Content as defined in the Standard Terms and remain
       the exclusive property of Vantage Logistics.

  H.2  Integration Support. Quillhub will provide up to forty (40) hours of
       integration support at no additional charge during the first six (6)
       months following the Effective Date, to assist Vantage Logistics with
       API integration into Vantage Logistics' warehouse management system.

  H.3  Non-Compete Clarification. The exclusivity in Section 4.1 applies
       only to the Route Optimizer product line. Quillhub may offer other
       products and services to any customer, including third-party logistics
       companies in Washington and Oregon, provided those products and services
       do not provide route optimization, fleet tracking, or delivery-window
       management functionality substantially similar to Route Optimizer.
