BUSINESS DESCRIPTION
--------------------
Quillhub Inc. develops and operates "Route Optimizer," a cloud-based logistics-
optimization platform. Route Optimizer provides real-time route planning, fleet
tracking, and delivery-window management for mid-market retail and logistics
companies. The platform is offered as a software-as-a-service product.

Quillhub does not own or operate data-center infrastructure. It relies on a
third-party hosting provider (currently StratoNode Inc.) for cloud infrastructure,
compute, storage, and network services.
